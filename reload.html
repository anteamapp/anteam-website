Freight Operators: AI-Powered Backloading to Cut Empty Miles

Anteam Reload™

Analyses routes to identify and fill empty legs automatically — turning unprofitable journeys into revenue.

Book a Demo →

Anteam Reload™ is backloading software that analyses planned routes and delivery schedules to identify inefficient empty legs. By detecting these gaps, the system automatically matches them with suitable backhaul orders from own or other suppliers, effectively filling unused capacity and maximising vehicle utilisation. This not only reduces wasted mileage and fuel consumption but also unlocks new revenue opportunities from journeys that would previously generate no return.
