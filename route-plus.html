Over 32% of Road Transport CO2 Comes From the Last Mile

Last-mile delivery is often just the final short leg of a much longer journey, but it accounts for over 32% of CO2 emissions in road transport. Despite that outsized impact, most fleets don't track last-mile emissions effectively, so the biggest opportunity for improvement is also the least visible one.

Anteam Route+™ closes that gap. It's patent-pending AI dynamic route insertion software that dynamically matches same-day delivery orders with existing or planned routes in real time, so a vehicle already on the road picks up more of the deliveries near it instead of running a separate, less efficient trip. Anteam Route+ has already been validated by a government backed pilot in collaboration with NHS hospitals and a national UK pharmacy chain. The dynamic route insertion software ran across 1000+ vehicles, running 1000+ routes a day; totaling more than 100,000 weekly deliveries.
