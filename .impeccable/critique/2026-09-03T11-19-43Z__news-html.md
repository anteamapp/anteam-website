#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The typographic system (Barlow Condensed headlines, IBM Plex body/mono, amber accent) is genuinely on-brand and doesn't read as generic SaaS. The failure isn't the visual language, it's that the page is unfinished: a `.sidebar-card`, `.related-card`, `.related-grid` system was designed and fully styled (lines 238–339) and then never placed in the HTML. That's the tell here, not gradients or rounded cards, it's a page that looks like a template with the content slot half-filled.

**Deterministic scan** (`npx impeccable detect`): 8 findings, all warnings.
- 2× `dark-glow`: zero-offset amber glow on `#reading-progress` (box-shadow blur, twice-counted for the two selector states). Small element, but it's the one spot on the page that reads as generic "AI tool" chrome rather than the site's own idiom.
- 5× `undersized-ui-text`: "Published"/"Author" meta labels (10px), the "35+" stat label (10.4px), and the comparison table's header + two body cells (10.4px) all sit below the 11px legibility floor.
- 1× `all-caps-body`: uppercase applied to the 30-char stat-callout label.
- 1× `layout-transition`: `transition: width` on the progress bar animates a layout property (minor, low-traffic element).

No false positives, all eight are real and specific to lines in the `<style>` block.

**Note**: browser visualization wasn't used for this run; findings above come from static source review plus the deterministic scanner. Re-run with `impeccable live` for the visual `[Human]` overlay if you want it pinned in-browser.

#### Overall Impression

The prose and typography are the best-executed part of the site's content register: specific, numbers-led, no hype, reads like the "grounded futurism" brand voice actually sounds. The problem is structural, not stylistic. This is called `news.html`, the nav labels it "News" as if it's a section, and it delivers exactly one article with no index, no related content, and no way back out. The biggest opportunity isn't polish, it's deciding what "News" actually is on this site before a second article makes the gap obvious.

#### What's Working
- **The comparison table and stat callout carry real numbers** ("35+ years", 10+ patents, the wrapper-vs-bespoke cost table) exactly per the "numbers earn trust" design principle, no vague AI-adjective claims.
- **Typography hierarchy is deliberate and consistent** with the rest of the site: Barlow Condensed for display, IBM Plex Mono for meta labels, clear weight/size steps between h2/h3/body.
- **Reduced-motion is respected** (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` disables the fade-up stagger), matching the accessibility principle already established sitewide.

#### Priority Issues

**[P1] The hero image is a visible stock placeholder, not real photography**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md's core visual differentiator is "real industrial photography (night highways, container terminals, delivery fleets)," explicitly *not* generic imagery. The hero here is `https://picsum.photos/seed/aicontrol3/1920/1080`, a random Lorem Picsum placeholder, and the source comment literally says "Filler hero image via picsum" (line 479). It's the first thing a visitor sees on an article arguing that Anteam builds bespoke, not generic.
- **Fix**: Swap in a real photo consistent with the industrial/HUD imagery used on quote.html and reload.html (control room, terminal, fleet).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish news.html`

**[P1] The page is a structural dead end with no news index or related content**
- **Why it matters**: The nav calls this section "News" (singular hub), but there's no listing of other posts, no breadcrumb, and no "back to news" path. Worse, the CSS for exactly this, `.sidebar-card`, `.sidebar-tag`, `.related-card`, `.related-grid`, `.related-card-img`, `.related-card-title` (lines 238–339), plus JS that resizes a `.related-grid` that doesn't exist in the DOM (lines 674–686), is fully built and dead. Right now this reads as an unfinished template, and the day a second article ships, there's nowhere for a reader to discover it.
- **Fix**: Either build a real news index at `news.html` (move this article to its own URL) or populate the already-styled related-articles grid with 2–3 other pieces (even cross-links to the product pages) so the page doesn't end abruptly.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable craft news index`

**[P1] Mobile nav is missing the "News" link**
- **Why it matters**: Desktop nav (line 449–453) lists Mission / Products / News. The mobile hamburger menu (line 468–471) only has Mission and Products, no News. A mobile visitor to any other page on the site cannot reach News from the menu at all, and there's no `nav-scrolled` breadcrumb here either to compensate.
- **Fix**: Add the News link to `#mobile-nav`, matching desktop.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit` (or a direct one-line fix)

**[P2] Functional text renders below the 11px legibility floor**
- **Why it matters**: The meta bar ("Published" / "Author" labels, 10px), the stat callout label (10.4px), and the entire comparison table, header row and two data cells (10.4px), are all under the floor. The table is the page's one data-forward moment (wrapper vs. bespoke cost comparison); making it borderline-illegible undercuts the "numbers earn trust" principle it exists to serve.
- **Fix**: Raise `.meta-item`, `.stat-callout-label`, and `.article-body th/td` font sizes to at least 11px.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset news.html`

**[P2] Zero-offset amber glow on the reading-progress bar**
- **Why it matters**: `box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(255,186,55,0.5)` on `#reading-progress` is the one spot on an otherwise disciplined page that reads as generic "AI tool" chrome rather than the site's own industrial idiom, flagged twice by the detector.
- **Fix**: Drop the glow or replace with a thin neutral border/no shadow.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish news.html`

#### Persona Red Flags

**The Skeptical Buyer** (ops lead comparing Anteam against Quotiss/Freightify, per PRODUCT.md's audience): Reads the wrapper-vs-bespoke argument, is convinced, wants proof this isn't a one-off blog post, looks for more content or a case study. Finds nothing: no related articles, no news index, only the two inline CTAs (product links, "Book a demo"). Bounces to the demo link or leaves; nothing captures the "not ready yet, show me more" reader.

**Time-pressed exec on mobile**: Opens the site on a phone, taps the hamburger menu to find "News" after seeing it mentioned somewhere, it's not there (only Mission/Products). Has to already know the URL to reach this page on mobile.

#### Minor Observations
- `text-transform: uppercase` on the "Years of Combined AI Experience" stat label (30 chars) is borderline for the all-caps-body rule; low severity but easy to fix by dropping the transform or shortening the label.
- `.article-hero-hud` dot-grid overlay and `.hud-corner-tl/br` decorations aren't used anywhere in this file's markup, dead CSS worth deleting or actually applying to the hero.
- No JSON-LD `Article` schema or Open Graph tags (`og:image`, `og:type`) despite this being shareable news content, worth a look given the site's SEO focus elsewhere.

#### Questions to Consider
- Is `news.html` meant to be a permanent single-article page, or the first of many? If the latter, the index/listing problem should be solved before article #2 ships, not after.
- The related-articles CSS is already written, what stopped it from being wired up: no second article to link to yet, or an oversight?
