# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Operations and commercial leads at freight forwarding, logistics, and industrial companies (also aerospace, energy, manufacturing, and wind, per the homepage) evaluating whether to adopt an AI tool for quoting, route optimisation, or backloading. They are B2B buyers, time-pressed, technically literate but not data scientists, and are comparing Anteam against named commercial competitors (e.g. Quotiss, Freightify, Shipthis for quoting). Their job to be done on a product page like quote.html is to quickly judge credibility and fit, then either book a demo or start a free trial.

## Product Purpose

Anteam builds industrial AI software (Route+™, Quote™, Reload™) that sits on top of a customer's existing systems to cut costs, improve efficiency, and reduce carbon across logistics and industrial operations. The website's job is to make each product's value and proof (real accuracy numbers, cost savings, named institutional partners) legible fast enough to convert a skeptical B2B visitor into a demo booking or trial signup. Success is measured in contact-form submissions and demo bookings, not time-on-page.

## Brand Personality

Industrial, precise, confident. The voice is terse and data-led (real stats, not adjectives), never hypey. Visually this shows up as a dark, amber-accented, HUD-inflected aesthetic paired with real industrial photography (night highways, container terminals, delivery fleets), what the site's own copy calls "grounded futurism": futuristic tooling grounded in real, physical, industrial work, not abstract sci-fi.

## Anti-references

No specific named anti-references given, but the brand should avoid generic SaaS visual cliches: soft pastel gradients, bouncy rounded card grids, and vague "AI-powered" claims without a number behind them. Anteam's differentiator from competitors is publishing real, sometimes uncomfortable numbers (measured accuracy, pilot results) rather than marketing adjectives.

## Design Principles

- Numbers earn trust: lead with measured, attributable stats (accuracy %, cost savings, CO2 reduction) over unquantified claims. If a number can't be sourced or attributed, soften it to an observation rather than presenting it as fact.
- Grounded futurism: industrial, real-world imagery and subtle HUD-style overlays signal proven deployment, not speculative AI fantasy.
- Data privacy as a trust signal: for tools trained on customer data (notably Quote), state plainly that a customer's data and supplier network are never pooled or shared with other customers.
- Practice what you preach: an industrial AI company's own site should read as precise and uncluttered, not noisy or over-decorated, mirroring the discipline it sells.
- Fast path to the ask: since the audience is time-pressed B2B, keep "Book a Demo" and the contact form within easy reach throughout, without turning every section into a CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No explicit requirements stated; default to WCAG AA. The site already respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (logo carousel, hero animations) and should keep doing so for any new motion.
