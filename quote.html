Manual Supplier Calls Are Slowing You Down

Most freight forwarders still build quotes the same way: calling round a list of suppliers one by one, waiting on replies, and piecing together a price by hand. It's slow, inconsistent between team members, and doesn't scale as order volume grows. Speed matters more than most forwarders realise, the first forwarder to quote generally wins the majority of opportunities, so every hour spent chasing suppliers manually is an hour a faster competitor can use to win the business instead.

Anteam Quote™ replaces that manual process. It's AI freight quoting software that learns exclusively from your own historical order data and your own suppliers, generating accurate, data-driven quotes and predicting suitable suppliers instantly for road, sea and air freight, across national and international lanes. Every quote returns up to 5 ranked supplier recommendations with a price estimate each, drawn entirely from your own order history, never a shared pool pulled from other companies using the tool.

FAQ

Common Questions

How accurate are Anteam Quote's price estimates?

In a live pilot with a leading UK freight forwarder, comparing 3 months of live orders, Anteam Quote achieved a Mean Absolute Error of 3.8% on price estimates, versus 20% MAE from a popular commercial alternative.

Is my supplier and pricing data shared with other companies?

No. Anteam Quote learns exclusively from your own historical order and supplier data. Your data is never shared with other companies, and the suppliers recommended to you are never shared with other customers of the tool.

What freight modes does Anteam Quote support?

Anteam Quote handles road, sea and air freight, across national and international shipping lanes, for as many different vehicle types as your company works with.
