## 30% of HGV Journeys Run Completely Empty

Roughly 30% of HGV journeys on the road today are driven with an empty trailer. That's a leg the truck was going to drive anyway, generating no revenue and no purpose, just fuel burned and emissions released for nothing. It's bad for profit and bad for the environment, and it happens because finding a suitable return load by hand takes time most dispatchers don't have.

Anteam Reload™ replaces that manual search. It's backloading software that matches live orders against a fleet's planned routes, looking for pickup and dropoff points close to the route already being driven, from your own fleet or from other suppliers you choose to work with. For each potential match, it calculates the detour that delivery would add and weighs it against the CO2 and revenue gained, so a dispatcher sees the tradeoff, not just a suggestion.

## Common Questions

### How much can backloading software reduce empty miles?

Anteam Reload has reduced empty legs by 50% and cut CO2 emissions by 34% per AI-matched delivery, while increasing profit by 16%.

### How does Anteam Reload decide which orders to match?

Reload checks live orders against a vehicle's planned route in real time. If a pickup or dropoff sits close enough to the route already being driven, it calculates the extra distance that delivery would add and weighs it against the CO2 and revenue gained before suggesting the match.

### Does Reload only match my own orders, or orders from other suppliers too?

Both. Reload matches empty legs with suitable orders from your own fleet or from other suppliers you choose to work with, whichever fills the gap best.
