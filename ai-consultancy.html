Consultants Who've Actually Shipped the Models

Most AI consultancy comes from one of two directions: a strategy firm that hands you a recommendation and leaves someone else to build it, or a vendor whose "consultation" always concludes you need their product. Neither is scoping your problem, they're scoping their own next step.

And neither tends to stick around for what happens after the recommendation, whether the model actually gets built, works, or quietly gets abandoned once the deck's been approved.

How We Work

From Consultation to Hosting

Anteam's applied AI consultancy doesn't stop at a recommendation. The same team can carry an engagement through the full lifecycle: the initial consultation, data analysis to see what's actually feasible on what you have, development and training of the model itself, and ongoing maintenance and hosting once it's live, so the model stays accurate as your operation changes instead of drifting the moment no one's watching it. You're not left holding a recommendation with no one who understands how to act on it, or a model with no one who understands how it was built.

This is run by the same team that builds and deploys the models, with over 35 years of combined AI experience and 10+ international patents across logistics, aerospace, manufacturing and energy. Not every problem needs a new model built from scratch. The consultation looks at your specific operation: where AI can realistically cut cost or risk, what data you'd actually need, and whether a bespoke model, a lighter-weight fix, or no AI at all is the right call, an honest answer even when it isn't the one that leads to a build.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you only recommend building an Anteam product?

No. The consultation scopes what your operation actually needs, whether that's a bespoke model, a lighter-weight fix, or a conclusion that AI isn't the right tool for this specific problem yet.

What if AI isn't the right fix for our problem?

We say so. Not every operational problem needs a new model built from scratch, and telling you that honestly is part of the consultation, not a failure of it.

What industries do you cover?

The team's applied experience spans logistics, aerospace, manufacturing, energy and wind, with over 35 years of combined AI experience and 10+ international patents across those sectors.

How is this different from a strategy consulting firm?

The team scoping the engagement is the same team that builds and deploys the models, not a separate group handing off recommendations to someone else to implement.

Do you just consult, or can you build and run it too?

Both. The engagement can cover the full lifecycle: consultation, data analysis, development and training of the model, and ongoing maintenance and hosting once it's live, so you're not left managing a model in-house with no one who understands how it was built.
